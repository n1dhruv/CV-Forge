# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Job seekers tailoring evidence-backed resumes to a job description.

## Product Purpose

Create one-page, ATS-compatible resumes from the user's saved evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Users bring their own LLM provider.
- Source evidence is immutable.
- AI changes require user review before application.
- Resume output must fit exactly one page.

## Evidence on Hand

Only user-provided profile details and source evidence may support resume claims. The product must not fabricate claims.

## Product Principles

- Preserve user ownership of source evidence.
- Make AI changes explicit and reviewable.
- Keep generated resumes grounded and one page.
